00. The High-Ticket AI Agency

You are not selling "AI Web Development." Clients don't care about the tools you use; they care about business outcomes. Sell the result: "I build booking systems that never double-book," or "I build client portals that reduce customer support calls by 50%."

High-Ticket Niche Examples:
  • Real Estate: "I build custom CRM dashboards for brokers to track leads." (Value: $3k - $8k)
  • Healthcare: "I build secure, HIPAA-compliant patient intake forms and portals." (Value: $5k - $12k)
  • E-Commerce: "I build headless storefronts that load in under 1 second." (Value: $4k - $10k)

01. Prospect Research & Audits

Do not let AI invent metrics. Feed it facts you manually gather (e.g., from Google Maps, broken links on their site, or poor mobile rendering) and let it generate a factual analysis.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.01 The Factual Website Audit
USE WHENEvaluating a prospect's current website.
RECOMMENDED ROLEStrong reasoning model
CONTEXT TO ATTACHScreenshots, manual notes, competitor URLs
DO NOT ATTACHFabricated SEO scores
RUN INSIDEAntigravity Agent or standard chat UI
NEXTPrompt 3.02 Outreach Message
Act as a website conversion and technical audit assistant. I am evaluating a potential freelance prospect. IMPORTANT: Do not invent statistics, PageSpeed scores, traffic numbers, conversion rates, rankings or business information. Only make factual claims supported by information I provide or evidence we actually inspect. PROSPECT: [Business Name] WEBSITE: [URL] BUSINESS TYPE: [Type] LOCATION: [Location] MY OBSERVATIONS: [Paste your manual observations here] Analyse the available evidence across: 1. First impression & visual consistency 2. Mobile usability 3. Navigation & Calls-to-action 4. Obvious technical problems Separate findings into: CONFIRMED OBSERVATIONS, POTENTIAL OPPORTUNITIES, THINGS THAT REQUIRE VERIFICATION. Identify the 3 strongest legitimate reasons I could contact this business.

02. AI-Assisted Outreach

Cold outreach fails because it sounds like AI. Force the model to sound human, direct, and focused strictly on the facts from your audit.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.02 Cold Email / LinkedIn DM
USE WHENGenerating the initial contact message.
RECOMMENDED ROLEFast model
CONTEXT TO ATTACH@audit-results
DO NOT ATTACHMarketing jargon, buzzwords
RUN INSIDEAntigravity Agent or standard chat UI
NEXTSend message, set CRM reminder
Draft a cold outreach message based on the Factual Audit results. Requirements: - Target: The business owner or manager. - Platform: [Email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp]. - Tone: Direct, respectful, zero marketing jargon. Do NOT use words like "synergy," "skyrocket," or "unlock." - Structure: 1. Quick, personalized hook based on the audit. 2. Point out ONE specific, factual problem (e.g., broken mobile booking). 3. Offer a very low-friction next step (e.g., a 5-minute loom video, or a quick question). Keep it under 4 sentences. The goal is a reply, not a sale.

03. Discovery Calls & Scope

After a successful call, you have a messy page of notes. AI is incredible at turning messy human thoughts into professional project scopes.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.03 Discovery Note Analysis
USE WHENPost-call processing.
RECOMMENDED ROLEStrong reasoning model
CONTEXT TO ATTACHRough call notes, prospect budget hints
DO NOT ATTACHTechnical implementation details
RUN INSIDEAntigravity Agent or standard chat UI
NEXTPrompt 3.04 Proposal Draft
Act as a senior project manager. I just had a discovery call with a prospect. Here are my raw, unedited notes: [PASTE NOTES] Please analyse these notes and extract: 1. The Core Business Goal (Why do they want this now?) 2. Required Features (Must-haves vs Nice-to-haves) 3. Pain points with their current solution. 4. Missing Information (What questions do I still need to ask them?) 5. Red Flags (Are there any scope-creep risks based on what they said?) Format this as a clean internal brief for me.
AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.04 The Client Proposal
USE WHENSending the official quote.
RECOMMENDED ROLEStrong reasoning model
CONTEXT TO ATTACH@internal-brief
DO NOT ATTACHNone
RUN INSIDEAntigravity Agent or standard chat UI
NEXTWait for client signature
Turn the internal brief into a professional client proposal. Include: - Executive Summary (Their goals). - Scope of Work (Deliverables: e.g., 5-page site, Supabase Auth, Admin Dashboard). - Exclusions (What is NOT included, very important). - Timeline estimates. - Investment (I will charge $XXXX for this). Keep the language professional but highly accessible. Do not use overly technical jargon. Explain features in terms of business value.

03.5 AI Project Management

Once the client signs, scope creep is your worst enemy. Use AI to generate strict milestone trackers and client update emails that keep everyone aligned.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.04b Weekly Client Update Email
COPY THIS INTO ANTIGRAVITY: Draft a weekly update email for my client. Project: [Project Name] What I did this week: [Bullet points of tasks completed] What I am doing next week: [Bullet points of upcoming tasks] Blockers: [Anything I need from the client] Keep it professional, concise, and reassuring. Do not use technical jargon. Emphasize that the project is on track.

04. Delivery, Handover & Retainers

The project isn't over when the code is written. A professional handover is what turns a one-off client into a recurring retainer.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.05 Pre-Delivery QA & Handoff
USE WHENFinal polish before sending to client.
RECOMMENDED ROLEFast model
CONTEXT TO ATTACH@project-scope @final-repo
DO NOT ATTACHNone
RUN INSIDEAntigravity Agent or standard chat UI
NEXTPrompt 3.06 Upsell Maintenance
I am about to hand over the completed web application to the client. First, cross-reference the original Scope of Work with the current codebase. Are there any promised features missing? Second, draft a 'Handover Email' for the client. It should include: - A link to the live production site. - Instructions on how to log in to the admin dashboard. - A request for them to review the site by [Date]. - A polite note that any requested changes beyond the original scope will be quoted separately.
AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.05b Loom Training Script Generator
COPY THIS INTO ANTIGRAVITY: I am recording a 5-minute Loom video to train the client on their new dashboard. Please write a short script/outline for me to follow. Features to cover: 1. How to log in securely. 2. How to view new leads. 3. How to update the content. Structure the script to be encouraging and clear. Remind them at the end that they can reach out for a retainer if they need ongoing help.

05. Selling the Retainer

Web applications require maintenance. Uptime monitoring, dependency updates, and minor feature tweaks. Offer this as a monthly recurring revenue (MRR) package.

AIWEBDEV PROMPT 3.06 Retainer Upsell Pitch
COPY THIS INTO ANTIGRAVITY: Draft an email pitching a monthly maintenance retainer to a client whose project I just completed. The retainer includes: - 24/7 Uptime monitoring (via Vercel/Render) - Monthly security dependency updates - Up to 2 hours of minor UI tweaks per month - Priority email support Frame this as "insurance for their new investment" rather than a technical maintenance plan. Offer it at [Price]/month.
